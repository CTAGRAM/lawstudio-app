LAWSTUDIO

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 August 2026

LawStudio is a private video-production tool used by our team to plan, produce, and publish explainer videos to our own YouTube channels. This policy explains what data the application handles and how.

Information we handle

  • Google account & YouTube data. When you connect a YouTube channel, Google provides an OAuth access token and refresh token, your channel's public details (name, thumbnail), and your account email.
  • Video content and metadata you create in LawStudio (topics, scripts, storyboards, rendered videos, titles, descriptions, tags).
  • Performance metrics (views, likes, comments, watch time) retrieved from the YouTube Data and YouTube Analytics APIs for videos on your connected channels.

How we use it

  • To upload, edit, and remove videos on your connected YouTube channels at your explicit request.
  • To display channel and video analytics inside the dashboard.
  • To improve future video plans based on what has performed well.

Storage & security

OAuth tokens are stored server-side in our database with access restricted to the application's server credentials; they are never exposed to the browser or to third parties. You can disconnect a channel at any time, which deletes its stored tokens.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your Google user data with any third party. Data is used solely to provide the features described above.

Limited Use disclosure. LawStudio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can revoke LawStudio's access to your Google account at any time via your Google Account permissions, or by disconnecting the channel inside LawStudio.
